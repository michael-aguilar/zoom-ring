ZOOM CONTROLLER WIRING V1 - CHECKED 2026-10-15

Scope: standard Seeed XIAO RP2040, Adafruit DRV8833 #3297 and Adafruit encoder motor #4639. Electrical documentation review, not a bench test or guarantee of lens torque suitability. Diagram pin names are functional connections, not a physical board footprint.

Checked:
- XIAO D4=GPIO6 and D5=GPIO7 are I2C0 SDA/SCL.
- D6=GPIO0 and D7=GPIO1 are PWM-capable; use as GPIO, not UART.
- D0=GPIO26 drives SLP. Initialize AIN1/AIN2 and SLP low. Enable SLP only with valid control input. Driver internally pulls SLP down.
- D2=GPIO28 and D3=GPIO29 are available digital inputs. Enable INPUT_PULLUP on both for encoder signals, consistent with vendor example. Set direction sign in firmware during unloaded test.
- XIAO GPIO is 3.3V only. Driver accepts 3.3V inputs at 5V motor supply. No level shifter needed for this exact motor powered as drawn.
- XIAO 5V pad and DRV8833 VM receive parallel branches from switched USB 5V; motor current does not pass through the XIAO. All grounds common.
- #4639 motor permits 4.5-6V motor supply and 3-5V encoder supply. Motor red/white go only to the two motor A bridge outputs. Encoder black=3.3V; blue=GND; yellow/green=signal. THESE ARE NOT GENERIC WIRE COLORS.
- C1 470uF >=10V electrolytic: positive to VM, negative to GND, near driver. C2 100nF ceramic across motor terminals, near motor. Board already has local bypass components.
- Two nominal 4.7k I2C pull-ups to 3.3V shown. Account for any built-in pull-ups; do not blindly duplicate a strong pull-up set. Start at 100kHz on the Nunchuck cable. Verify clone pinout and communication; color and initialization behavior can vary.
- External 5V and computer USB must not be connected simultaneously to the standard XIAO. Unplug the external supply before programming; do not run the motor from the PC USB port during this process.
- VM solder pad is not reverse-polarity protected. The board's separate VMotor/terminal input is protected. Diagram intentionally names VM, not a generic VCC input.
- Unused B motor outputs, BIN1/BIN2 and FLT may remain unconnected for this setup; B inputs have internal pull-downs. Existing sense resistors stay installed unless deliberately changed.

Still requires build testing:
- Motor torque, pulley ratio, minimum smooth speed, gear noise, mechanical end stops, and belt tension.
- Current limit: stock A-channel Rsense=0.2 ohm gives nominal 1A. This is not an appropriate lens torque limit. Choose a lower limit by replacing the A sense resistor; I_limit=0.2/Rsense. 1.0 ohm yields nominal 0.2A as a calculation example, not a certified lens-safe value. Adding a resistor in parallel increases the limit. Keep the bypass jumper open.
- Firmware needs input timeout, enable/dead-man behavior, acceleration/deceleration, encoder stall detection, and travel limits initialized from a known position. Motor encoder does not measure belt slip or absolute lens position after power cycling. Do not automatically home by driving into the lens stop.
- A USB pack must stay on at idle load; verify it does not automatically shut off. Actual supply/noise and clone behavior have not been bench tested.
